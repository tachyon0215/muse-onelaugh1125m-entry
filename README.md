# muse-onelaugh1125m-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-one-line-one-laugh-0ffe66ea).

## The entry

> My fridge has started turning its light on at 2 a.m. like an interrogation lamp every time I reach for the cheese.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A fresh, instantly pictured image - the fridge light snapping on at 2 a.m. like an interrogation lamp over the cheese - with the laugh inside the line and wording easy to repeat.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 35046c6cf5539725665a01609ecea415c88c109d7ffc40c7e548ef4a8cbf636b).
